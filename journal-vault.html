Why writing heals

You do not need to be a writer. You do not need good spelling or full sentences. You just need to be honest with a page that will not judge you.

  • Naming a feeling softens it. “I feel awful” is a fog. “I feel ashamed about what I said” is something you can hold. Putting a name on it often takes some of the sting out.
  • It gets the loop out of your head. Thoughts that go round and round at 3am lose some of their grip once they are on the page. You can put them down and leave them there.
  • You start to see patterns. Over weeks, you may notice what tends to come before a bad day — and what tends to help. That is your own wisdom, written by you.
  • It is a record of how far you have come. On the hard days, you can scroll back and see that you have been here before, and you got through it.

Not sure where to start?

Pick one. Answer it in a single line if that is all you have today. One line is enough.

  • Right now I feel… and I think it is because…
  • What is taking up the most room in my head today?
  • One small thing that went okay today was…
  • What would I say to a friend who felt the way I do?
  • Something I got through that I once thought I could not…
  • What do I need right now — rest, food, water, a person, or quiet?
  • What should tomorrow-me know?

Our privacy promise, in plain words

This is yours. Your words are sacred, and we treat them that way. Here is exactly how the vault works — nothing more, nothing less.

  • Your notes are saved only in this browser, on this device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot read them.
  • They are not linked to an account. Signing in does not copy them anywhere. They will not appear on your other phones or computers.
  • Anyone who uses this device and browser could open this page. If you share a phone, keep that in mind. A screen lock helps.
  • Clearing your browser data will delete them. So will private or incognito browsing once you close the window. Use Export under “More” to keep your own copy as a text file.
  • You can delete everything, any time. Open “More” and type FORGET. It is gone from this device for good.
