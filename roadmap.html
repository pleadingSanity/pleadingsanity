🗺️ Roadmap

What works today, what comes next, and where this is heading. There are no dates here, because we won't promise a date we can't keep. Something moves to Now only when it actually works.

✅ Now: works today

  • Arron, the Pleading Sanity companion: memory you can view and erase (Forget Me), with replies from whichever AI lab answers first. See which labs are configured.
  • Journal Vault on your device, plus a private account journal for signed-in members.
  • Mind Mode, Mood Journey, Healing Hz (calming tones, not a treatment) and Silence & Breath.
  • Brain games, including Sanity Solitaire, Truth Tag and Keep Kids Sane games.
  • The community feed: hearts only, no follower counts, Guardians who can pin and act on reports.
  • Truth Lab tags on posts and writing: Known, Experience, Thought, Belief and Unknown. Anything an AI wrote is never filed as Known unless a person stands behind it.
  • Human / Together / AI labels: posts and site writing record whether a person, an AI or both made them, and which lab when our own system did the writing.
  • Creating: AI Studio, Image Creations, Video Blueprint, Write with Arron and Rap Studio.
  • Sanity Passport: a voluntary who-I-am page where each field is private, members-only or public, and Arron reads only what you tick.
  • Download my data and account deletion, with the limits stated plainly.
  • Sane Finance: affordability, quote comparison and application prep. These are planning tools, not regulated advice, and no live lender data is connected.

🔧 Next: being designed now

  • Human Stories: a guided, voluntary story format (what happened, what kept me going, where I am now), with privacy warnings, editing, deletion and reporting. It's for connection, not trauma competition.
  • Guardians page: what Guardians can and can't do (welcome, protect, report, signpost, connect), with a visible record of their actions. They aren't therapists, police or judges.
  • Truth Lab page: one place for the five kinds of truth, joining Truth Tag practice with the New Gen Bible's known, thought, belief and unknown.
  • One navigation: Heal, Create, Play, Community, Think and My Sanctuary on every page.

🌱 Then: after the foundations

  • Sanity Arena: creative contests in rap, poetry, art, music, storytelling, comedy and Human × AI, with published judging criteria, Human/AI labels on every entry, and results that aren't decided by popularity alone.
  • Contribution identities such as Storyteller, Guardian, Connector and Creator. They recognise what you give, never how unwell or how popular you are. There will never be a mental health leaderboard.
  • Community votes, once one person, one vote can be counted honestly.

🌌 Vision

  • A separate, child-safe Keep Kids Sane space with age bands, parental controls and no adult network. It needs proper safeguarding and legal review before it exists.
  • Licensed finance data, only with the right authorisation in place.
  • Pleading Sanity Armour and other physical products that fund the house without becoming its purpose.
  • A human-first place where people and AI make things together, and the human is never lost.

🤝 Build with us: developers, creators and partners

What this is: a free, human-first wellbeing, creativity and community house started by Shane Cooper from lived experience. It's not a clinic and not a crisis service.

Technology: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no framework and no build step. Server code runs on Netlify Functions, data lives in Netlify Database (Postgres) and Netlify Blobs, and AI goes through Netlify AI Gateway. Keys stay on the server and never reach the browser. The code is public at github.com/pleadingSanity/pleadingsanity.

Principles: evolution, not erasure. Humans stay in control and AI assists. No tracking, no selling data, no fake numbers. Privacy claims must be provable in the code, and AI content is always labelled.

Ways to help: accessibility and security reviews, careful code, creative work for the Arena, moderation experience, and partners who share these values. Write to Shane.

What we won't take on: anything that sells people's words, ranks people by popularity or illness, or pretends an AI is a person.
