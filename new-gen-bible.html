What it actually is

The New Gen Bible is NOT a book of rules someone else made up. It is a living, breathing philosophy for anyone who's ever felt like they don't fit.

It asks the questions most people are too scared to speak out loud:

  • Who are we, underneath all the labels?
  • Why are we conscious? What is this feeling of being alive?
  • What connects us — really connects us — beyond borders and beliefs?
  • What is truth? What is just someone's opinion? What do we still not know?
  • Why do we hurt each other when we're all just trying to survive?
  • Can we evolve — truly evolve — without erasing who we've been?
  • What happens when humans AND AI learn together instead of fighting?
  • How do we build a world where no one has to feel alone at 3am?
Nobody gets to own the truth.
Not a book. Not a leader. Not a nation. Not an AI. Not Shane.
We are ALL just looking. And that's okay.

Religions hold wisdom. Science reveals wonders. Philosophy challenges us. Ancient traditions carry memory. New technology opens doors. But NONE of that gives ANYONE the right to say "I am right and you are nothing."

Investigate. Question. Think for yourself. But don't think for everyone else.

The four levels — how we actually know

Before we say anything — we get honest about where it comes from:

Known

Proven, observed, shared reality. What we can all see, test, agree on.

Thought

Idea, vision, possibility, what makes sense to someone. Worth exploring, not claiming as fact.

Believed

Personal truth, faith, spiritual experience. Yours alone. Not everyone's rule.

Unknown

The great mystery. What we haven't figured out YET. And that's beautiful.

"Do we know this — or do we believe this?"
Ask that question. Always. Including of me. Including of this page. Including of any AI.

This lets us talk about God, consciousness, life after death, frequencies, AI, the stars — WITHOUT pretending certainty where there isn't any. No more "this is the only way." Just "this is what I see. What do you see?"

On this house, the soft tones live on Healing Hz. They are not claimed as certainty. Known, thought, believed, unknown — that split stays.

My story — not a hero. A survivor.

In my own words, exactly as I've lived it:

I'm not a prophet. I'm not a saint. I'm not perfect. I'm just Shane — and I've been through things that would break most people.

My bloodline

The dark places

I've been locked away — not because I was dangerous, but because I was hurting and no one knew what else to do. I've been medicated until I couldn't feel myself. I've been given labels that tried to shrink who I am. Professionals meant well. But they were reading about it. I was LIVING it.

I've fought the voices in my head too. The ones that say you're nothing. That you're a burden. That it would be easier to stop. I learned to sit with them. Not fight them away — understand them.

The realisation

They told me I was broken. I decided I was awake. I see things people ignore. I feel things people numb. I care too much, too deeply, too loudly. And that's not a flaw — that's the fire that lights this place.
