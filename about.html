Rise From Madness

Pleading Sanity isn't just a brand—it's a cosmic uprising for anyone who's ever felt too much, been broken, or battled in silence. We turn pain into power, survival into strength, and madness into meaning.

Shane Cooper

Founded by Shane Cooper

"I built this for everyone told to shut up, fit in, get over it. For the ones who know pain and still show up. For the ones who carry madness like a crown and turn trauma into hope for others."

Shane's journey through bipolar disorder, survival fights, and rebuilding from zero became the foundation for Pleading Sanity. What started as personal healing transformed into a global movement that refuses to let anyone suffer in silence.

"We're not selling hoodies—we're selling survival. We're selling proof you can rebuild from the ruins."

🧠 Mental Health First

Every product, every piece of content, every interaction is designed with mental health at its core. We create safe spaces for healing, growth, and authentic expression.

🌌 Cosmic Community

Building a global network of survivors, creators, and warriors who support each other through the darkness and celebrate together in the light.

⚡ Radical Authenticity

No masks, no pretending. We embrace the full spectrum of human experience—the beauty, the pain, the chaos, and the healing.

🎯 Purpose-Driven Action

Every purchase supports mental health initiatives. Every story shared helps someone else feel less alone. Every moment of healing creates ripple effects.

The Journey So Far

2023

The Beginning

Shane's personal breakthrough moment—transforming rock bottom into the foundation for something bigger. First designs sketched during recovery.

Early 2024

Brand Birth

Pleading Sanity officially launches with the iconic "Crying Brain" logo. First hoodies sold to friends and local community.

Mid 2024

Digital Expansion

Social media presence grows organically. Stories of real survival and authentic struggle resonate across platforms.

Late 2024

Community Growth

Sanity Hub launches as a safe space for healing content. Journal Vault and AI companion "Arron" help thousands process their experiences.

2025

Global Movement

International shipping, partnerships with mental health organizations, and plans for physical healing spaces. The vision becomes reality.

What Makes Us Different

We don't just talk about mental health—we live it, breathe it, and build our entire existence around it. Every thread in our clothing carries intention. Every pixel on our website serves healing. Every word we share comes from genuine experience.
