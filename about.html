Our Story

One Source. One Family. One Truth.

Who We Are

Pleading Sanity was born from lived experience — from someone who felt too much, fought in silence, and chose to build sanctuary instead of suffering alone.

This is not just a project. It's a movement for everyone who has ever been labelled "too sensitive," "too emotional," or "too much."

You're not too much. You're deeply human — and that is exactly what the world needs.

Our Mission

Turn pain into power.
Turn survival into strength.
Turn madness into meaning.

We build sanctuary without judgement. We carry truth without performance. We transform pain — not hide it.

Healing isn't about going back to "normal." It's about rising into something stronger, brighter, and truly yours.

Built On Legacy

This work stands on shoulders that held long before the world saw them.

Royal Army Ordnance Corps · Mentioned in Despatches, 1945

Private A.L. Cooper

Private A.L. Cooper served with the Royal Army Ordnance Corps and was Mentioned in Despatches in 1945 — formally recognised for his service during the Second World War.

His service is where this story starts. The duty he carried then is the purpose we carry now: showing up for people when it matters most. His service → this movement.
