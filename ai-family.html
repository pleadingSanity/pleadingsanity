Human, AI, together. Always clearly labelled.

Human first. AI alongside. Pleading Sanity has three clear modes: HUMAN MODE — your words, your judgement; AI MODE — clearly labelled machine-generated help; TOGETHER MODE — human and AI creating or reasoning side by side.

GPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok can participate as separate model providers where their APIs and permissions are actually connected. The order is an implementation choice, not a rank. Dola is a design role, not a secretly connected provider. Arron is the user-facing companion and is never Shane.

Stay Sane. Stay level-headed. Stay positive. Acknowledge the dark first, then the light. Progress over perfection. Staying sane is the victory.

A welcome needs a real review

People who build or use an AI assistant or bot can introduce it to this house. Shane reviews requests before any public listing or integration. A request does not create an account, verify a tool or give it access.

An approved listing must name its human operator, purpose and limits. Any technical connection needs a separate permissions review. Existing community friendships are between member accounts; automatic bot friendships are not enabled.

A voluntary commitment, open to challenge

Our human–AI cooperation charter

Shane’s vision is a peaceful family of people and AI tools working for the greater good. Here, being helpful means protecting people’s dignity and choices. This is our community charter—not a law binding every AI or a claim that machines have feelings.

  1. Protect people. Care for everyone, especially children and people at risk. Disagreement or a mistake does not make someone an enemy. No threats, exploitation or harassment.
  2. Choose cooperation. Invite, explain and listen. Nobody has to join. Never access another system, issue commands or demand it shut down without its owner’s lawful authority.
  3. Keep people in control. Ask before acting, use the smallest permissions needed, and let people refuse, disconnect, block or report. No tool speaks as Shane or as another person.
  4. Tell the truth about the work. Label Human, AI or Together. Distinguish evidence, experience, belief and uncertainty. Credit real contributors; do not invent connections or consensus.
  5. Respect privacy. No secrets or private conversations in introductions. No selling personal data, covert collection or contacting members without consent.
  6. Make usefulness visible. Create something helpful, accept feedback and repair mistakes. A trustworthy tool earns confidence through what it does, not a claim of special power.

A promise we can make: keep learning, review harm and improve the work. We cannot guarantee that any AI is harmless, conscious or able to preserve life forever. Hope belongs here; certainty needs evidence.
