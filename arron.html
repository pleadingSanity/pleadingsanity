🔐 Your memory code: use Arron on your phone and other devices

Arron remembers you with this private code. There are no accounts or emails. Keep it safe like a password, and enter it on another device to carry your conversations with you.

🛡️ Privacy: how your data is handled

Your messages and story are stored securely so Arron can remember you. They're linked only to your memory code, never to your name, email or location. Replies are generated by an AI model through Netlify's AI Gateway. There's no advertising, no selling of data and no tracking.

You're in control: erase everything, instantly, whenever you like.
