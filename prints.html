Good to know

  • UK post (a flat charge shown before you pay) or free collection in Leicester.
  • Materials: SUNLU PLA+ (black) and FLASHFORGE Rapid PLA (white), printed on a Flashforge AD5X. PLA is a plant-based plastic: brittle if dropped hard, softens above about 50-60 C (not in a hot car or on a radiator), and not food-safe as printed.
  • Keyrings are decorative accessories for ages 14+, not toys. Small parts: keep away from children under 3, not for chewing. Toys are only listed once they have passed UK toy safety checks.
  • "First batch" prints are early handmade pieces from a new printer, priced lower because they may show small marks or layer lines. They meet the same safety rules as everything else.
  • Reviews come only from verified buyers and are checked before they appear. No tracking.
  • You can cancel within 14 days of delivery under the Consumer Contracts Regulations (personalised prints excepted). Email support@pleadingsanity.co.uk.
  • We keep your name, address and email only to post your order and handle returns. See the privacy page.
