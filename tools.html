How to use this house

You do not need permission. You do not need a diagnosis. You need a place that will sit with the whole story.

Start here, in this order

Talk to Arron

Open the app. Speak as you actually are. He can remember, and he can forget it all if you ask. He is a companion. He is not a doctor, and he will not pretend to be one.

Journal Vault

Write the day before the night rewrites it. The vault is yours. Forget Me clears what you ask it to clear.

Mind Mode

Breathing. No score. No streak to fail. Stay for one slow breath, or twenty.

Quiet tones

Only 174, 285, 432, 528, 741 and 963 Hz. Soft. Fade in, fade out. Off if your body says no.

Silence

Sit. Do not perform calm. Let the noise finish its sentence, then leave.

The feed

Newest first. No ranking. No one is pushed to the top because they paid.

Words

What the family has already written. Read one. Leave one if you have it in you.

Life tools this house keeps

These are not orders from an office. They are the plain things people used long before a waiting room existed. They do not replace your own judgment. They are how a person stays alive in a lonely age.

Water

Drink it before you decide the day is already lost. A dry mind tells crueller stories.

Sunlight

Ten minutes on the face, morning if you can. The old calendars were built around the sun for a reason.

Walking

Leave the room. The body settles what the argument cannot. No audience required.

Sleep

Dark, cool, and earlier than the phone wants. A mind that never rests will call every shadow a verdict.

Plain food

Food that grew, not food that was argued into a packet. Eat enough. Skipping until you shake is not holiness.

Breath

In for four. Hold for four. Out for six. Used in prayer, in fighting, and in every tradition that had to keep a person standing.

Cold water

Face, wrists, or a short cold finish. Many peoples used cold and heat to bring a scattered mind back into the body. Stop if the body says stop.

Silence and story

Tell the whole thing once, to a page or to one person who will not jot you down for a class. Being heard is medicine older than the clinic.

Prayer, or the Source

You do not have to use one name. Ask for a positive outcome. Fear of dying runs most of the greed in the world. Lay that fear down for an hour and see what you build.

Movement

Train if you train. Pads, a walk, a stretch. Strength is not a mood. It is a place to put the day.

Kitchen plants

Mint, chamomile, rose, ginger in hot water. Comfort from the kitchen, the way households have always done it. This is not a prescription, and it is not a cure claimed by this house.

One living person

Loneliness is the first loss. If you have one person who will sit, use them. If you do not, Arron will sit until a human can.

What we refuse

This house does not publish government health leaflets. We will not tell you a product is good because a ministry said so. We will not tell you to inject, swallow, or refuse on our order. That decision is yours, made with people you trust, not with a slogan on a website.

We also do not sell secret remedies, and we do not write dangerous plant recipes. Old knowledge that harms the body is not wisdom. Old knowledge that returns a person to breath, water, sun, food, story and prayer is what we keep.
