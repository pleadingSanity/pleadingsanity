Website analytics and your choices

Some pages currently include Google Analytics, a Google service used to understand site visits and usage. When active, it may receive information about your device, browser and interactions and process it under Google's own terms. We are reviewing where this tracking loads and how consent should work.

Google Analytics is not loaded on our crisis, support or sanctuary pages, on Arron, on the journal, notes or finance pages, or on sign-in, sign-up, password reset, profile, settings, posting or community account pages. This privacy page does not load it either.

There is not yet a site-wide analytics consent control, so other public pages, such as games and general information pages, may still load it. Please do not assume those pages are analytics-free. You can adjust tracking protection in your browser. We do not intentionally include your private journal text in analytics events.

We will update this notice when the analytics implementation changes.

Arron and the AI providers

When you message Arron, your words are sent to our server and may be passed to the AI provider that answers. The main order is GPT through OpenAI, Claude through Anthropic, Gemini through Google, then Grok through xAI when Grok is configured.

If those four providers cannot answer, Arron gives you a short reply with the real help lines instead. Free fallback models routed through OpenRouter are switched off for Arron chats and the finance helper until their data-handling terms have been reviewed. If that is ever switched on, this page will say so first, and OpenRouter and the selected model provider would also process the message. These providers are separate services; Pleading Sanity does not control their systems or their own data-handling terms.

Keeping a chat "on this device" means it is not saved in our database. It does not keep the conversation on your device alone: each message is still sent to the AI provider that answers it.

We do not sell your words. However, this is not end-to-end encryption, and your message is not hidden from the AI service that processes it. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or information you would not want processed by these providers. You can use the local tools without chatting to Arron.

The Sane Finance GBT

Your finance profile, quotes, application tracker, saved brief and documents are kept in this browser's storage on your own device. Pleading Sanity has no copy and does not send them to any lender, broker or third party.

If you press "Ask the family", your question and any quote you paste are sent to our server and on to the AI providers we use (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and xAI) to write the answer. Your profile figures are only included if you tick the box first. We do not store the question or the answer, and we do not write them to our logs.

To stop people flooding the service, we count requests per visitor using a one-way scrambled (hashed) form of the network address, in a counter that resets every minute. No raw addresses are stored.

"Download my finance data" gives you a copy. "Delete all my finance data" on the finance page removes it from this device straight away. Clearing your browser data does the same.

The Sane Finance GBT is an education and preparation tool. It is not a lender, credit broker or financial adviser and is not authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority.
