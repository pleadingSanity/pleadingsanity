Arron and the AI providers

When you message Arron, your words are sent to our server and may be passed to the AI provider that answers. The main order is GPT through OpenAI, Claude through Anthropic, Gemini through Google, then Grok through xAI when Grok is configured.

If those four providers cannot answer, Arron may try a free fallback model routed through OpenRouter. If that fallback is used, OpenRouter and the selected model provider also process the message. These providers are separate services; Pleading Sanity does not control their systems or their own data-handling terms.

We do not sell your words. However, this is not end-to-end encryption, and your message is not hidden from the AI service that processes it. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or information you would not want processed by these providers. You can use the local tools without chatting to Arron.

The Sane Finance GBT

Your finance profile, quotes, application tracker, saved brief and documents are kept in this browser's storage on your own device. Pleading Sanity has no copy and does not send them to any lender, broker or third party.

If you press "Ask the family", your question and any quote you paste are sent to our server and on to the AI providers we use (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and xAI) to write the answer. Your profile figures are only included if you tick the box first. We do not store the question or the answer, and we do not write them to our logs.

To stop people flooding the service, we count requests per visitor using a one-way scrambled (hashed) form of the network address, in a counter that resets every minute. No raw addresses are stored.

"Download my finance data" gives you a copy. "Delete all my finance data" on the finance page removes it from this device straight away. Clearing your browser data does the same.

The Sane Finance GBT is an education and preparation tool. It is not a lender, credit broker or financial adviser and is not authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority.
