What this feed is

This is a feed of real people. Survivors, carers, friends, people having a hard week and people having a good one. Everything here was shared by someone who chose to be honest.

  • Newest first. No algorithm. Nothing is pushed up because it is loud or angry. You see posts in the order they were shared.
  • Hearts only. Never downvotes, never dislikes. We don’t feed darkness. If a post moves you, give it a heart. If it doesn’t, simply scroll on.
  • “X have walked this path.” Each post shows how many people have read it. So even when nobody replies, you know you were heard.
  • Arron marks the milestones. When a post reaches 10, 50, 100, 500 or 1,000 hearts, Arron speaks a few words to the person who wrote it. Others see a gentle glow on the post, but only the author sees their exact heart count. No one is ranked. No one is compared.

If you are carrying something heavy right now, you do not have to scroll through it alone. Read the house truth or see how to use this house.

Daily light

A few words from Arron to carry with you today.

  • You do not have to carry today and tomorrow at the same time. Just today. Just this hour, if that is all you can manage.
  • Small steps still move you. A shower, a glass of water, a text back — they all count.
  • Healing is not a straight line. Bad days are part of the path, not proof you have left it.
  • Storms are loud, but they always pass. You have outlasted every one so far.
  • Pain told honestly is the start of power. Evolution, not erasure.
