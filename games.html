    🧭 About the games — what each one is for

    These are small, kind games, not treatment. They can help you steady your focus, settle your breathing or give a busy mind somewhere softer to rest for a few minutes. Take what helps and leave the rest.

    🃏 Sanity Solitaire
    Three peaks, Klondike, FreeCell and Pyramid. Night cards, a book on the phone, and a friend seat that passes the same phone. Sound and music stay off until you ask.
    ♠️ Night Table
    Hold'em with play chips only. No cash pot, no rake, no withdraw. A friend link deals the same cards on both phones. The bets stay on each phone. A gift goes to the shop.
    🌌 Cosmic Connect
    Match pairs of cosmic cards, or join the stars to reveal a hopeful message. There is no timer, so it gently exercises memory while letting you go at your own pace.
    🏷️ Truth Tag
    Practise sorting statements into evidence, lived experience or philosophy, with a kind explanation after each one. It helps you read posts here, and things you see online, with a clearer and calmer eye.
    🧠 Cosmic Focus
    Watch a sequence light up, then repeat it back; the sequences grow slowly as you go. It is a gentle workout for short-term memory and focus, and a way to practise staying with one thing.
    🔢 Number Nebula
    Number patterns, sequences and bite-sized mental maths that scale with you. It is designed to build confidence with numbers rather than stress, one small win at a time.
    🌈 Pattern Galaxy
    Spot the shape, colour or shift that comes next. It trains visual attention and pattern-spotting, and many people find the noticing itself rather calming.
    🧩 Memory Ocean
    A calm pair-matching game with no timers and no rush. It gives your memory some light exercise while you breathe slowly and let the rest of the day wait.
    🎵 Rhythm Resonance
    Listen to a short run of soft notes and play it back. Listening closely draws your attention into the present moment, which can help when your thoughts are racing.
    🌠 Stardust Dash
    A quick, silly reflex game: catch the stars and dodge worries like “Did I leave the oven on?” It is there for a laugh and a change of gear when your mood needs lifting.
    🎧 Healing Hz
    Not a game so much as a place to listen: ten soft tones, from 174 Hz to 963 Hz. Some people find a gentle tone helps them slow down; we make no medical claims.
    🫧 Mind Mode
    Guided breathing with a glowing orb, a 5-4-3-2-1 grounding walk-through and gentle affirmations. It is for the moments you feel overwhelmed and want to come back to your body and the room around you.
    🌱 Mood Journey
    A private eight-week check-in: eight stepping stones, in your own words. Looking back over the weeks can help you notice patterns in your mood, and there is no pressure if you miss one.

    💡 Why This Matters — & How It Grows

    Every game built for whole-person health — memory → logic → perception → calm → vibration.
    No addiction tricks, no paywalls, no timers breathing down your neck. Just you and your mind, gently growing stronger.
