      To every artist we feature

      First and foremost — thank you. Your music got us through when nothing else could.

      We don't own your music. We don't claim to. We're not selling it. We're not running adverts over it. We're just a place where people who need to hear it can find it — the way you made it.

      If this station ever generates any income, you get a generous share for every piece of your work we feature. If you have concerns, questions, or want it removed — message Shane. No questions asked.

      Email: pleadingsanity1@gmail.com

      — Shane Cooper, Founder · Pleading Sanity / Insane Radio

      And to the stations who let us stream them — thank you

      Every live station on this page is run by someone else. SomaFM and Radio Paradise are listener-supported and keep their streams open on purpose so places like this can exist. Reprezent and Rinse are community and independent broadcasters who let London's underground reach the world. GotRadio, 181.FM and Urbanradio.com run independent web streams. Capital XTRA is a commercial national station whose public stream we link to directly.

      We don't host a single second of their audio. We point your browser straight at their official stream. If you love what you hear, back them — the links are on every card. Without them, this page is just a nice layout.

      — Shane Cooper, Founder · Pleading Sanity / Insane Radio

      How to back these stations

      These stations keep free radio alive. None of them pay us, and we don't host their audio — your browser streams directly from them. Here is exactly how to support each kind:

      • SomaFM — listener-supported, no ads. Donate at somafm.com/support (one-off or monthly).
      • Radio Paradise — listener-supported since 2000. Donate at radioparadise.com/support.
      • Reprezent Radio — South London community broadcaster. Support and volunteer via reprezent.org.
      • Rinse FM — independent London station. Back them via rinse.fm.
      • GotRadio, 181.FM, Urbanradio.com — independent web radio. Visiting their sites and listening helps cover stream costs; support links are on each card.
      • Capital XTRA / Capital XTRA Reloaded — commercial national stations (Global), funded by advertising. No donation page; info at capitalxtra.com.
      • BBC stations are licence-fee funded and are not included here.
