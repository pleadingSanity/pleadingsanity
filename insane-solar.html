Useful answers

Questions people actually ask

Do I need a south-facing roof?

No. East- and west-facing roofs may still be suitable. A site-specific design and generation estimate are needed.

Can I add a battery later?

Often, but compatibility, inverter choice, wiring and costs should be considered at the design stage.

Will my panels work during a power cut?

Most standard grid-connected systems shut down during a power cut. Backup operation requires specifically designed equipment.

Do you provide instant guaranteed prices?

No. Any initial estimate must be checked against roof condition, access, shading, electrical design and grid requirements.

Can I do everything by email?

Yes, you can begin and discuss your enquiry by email. A site survey may still be necessary before an accurate quote or installation.

Who provides the certificates?

Relevant installers, electricians, network operators and certification bodies issue or approve the documents within their remit. AI may help organise paperwork but cannot certify an installation.
