Why Us

Why a salesman shouldn't run a construction company.

Most solar firms are sales offices with a crew subbed out the back. We're a fitting crew that let software eat the office. Here's the real difference it makes to your roof — and to the lads who climb it.

Salesmen don't care about the people who actually fit the product. They don't know who's worth what. They cut costs in all the wrong places because they know nothing about how the job is done — only how to sell it. Having money and being able to sell isn't god for real grafters. Good workers go unnoticed, then the company cuts costs on the wrong workers. The lads who climb get shafted, and your roof pays for it.

We do it different: salesmen sell, surveyors survey, fitters fit, AI runs the office — everyone in their lane, no one cuts corners on the lads who climb.

The salesman-run model

  • ✕Undersold jobs → corners cut. Quoted cheap to win, then the lads are told to "make it work" with thinner rail or unbranded clips.
  • ✕Wrong kit specified — because the person pricing it has never held a rail or sized a string.
  • ✕No idea how long installs really take. Promised 1 day, actually 3 — your house is a building site all week.
  • ✕Blaming the lads when it goes wrong. The salesman who promised the impossible is long gone; the installer takes the heat.
  • ✕Chasing deposit over quality. Once your 25% clears, urgency drops off a cliff.
  • ✕Price-matched to win, then value-engineered down. You signed for Tier-1; you're getting whatever's cheapest in the van.

Energy Providers & Tariffs

Get paid for the power you don't use.

We're independent — we don't push a tariff. Here are the real UK SEG (Smart Export Guarantee) rates and solar-friendly tariffs worth looking at. All figures typical 2026 — verify at sign-up, subject to eligibility and an MCS-certified install; rates move with the market.

With a battery

Go for an Octopus Flux-style time-of-use tariff: charge overnight cheap (~5p), export at peak 4–7pm for ~27–30p/kWh typical. Battery pays for itself faster.

Without a battery

You export in daylight, so a flat SEG rate is what matters. EDF ~15p fixed if you're their customer; otherwise Octopus 4.1p flat is open to everyone. Switching is free and takes ~2 weeks.

We handle it

The AI office prepares your switch + SEG signup paperwork — you authorise and sign it, the supplier still decides. MPAN setup, export meter registration and the application, all after install. No downtime; your new supplier manages the switch.

Plain-English glossary

kWp = peak power your panels can produce (size of system). kWh = energy actually generated/used over time (what you pay for). A 4kWp system makes ~3,000–4,000 kWh/yr.

Microgeneration Certification Scheme — the kitemark that proves your install meets UK standards. Required for SEG payments and most warranties. We're registered; AI files the cert.

Grid connection rules. G98 (≤3.68kW per phase): fit then notify DNO within 28 days. G99 (bigger): pre-approval needed, ~45 working days. We handle the application.

Smart Export Guarantee — suppliers pay you for solar you export to the grid. Typical 3–15p/kWh flat, up to ~27–30p peak with a battery on smart tariffs. Needs MCS + smart meter.

Converts panel DC to house AC AND has battery charging built in. Add a battery later without changing the inverter. What we recommend if you're "maybe" on battery.

A battery that connects on the AC side (after your existing inverter). Used when you already have solar and retro-fit a battery. Slightly less efficient than DC-coupled but no inverter swap.

A smart meter that throttles your inverter so you never export more than your DNO allows. Required on some G99 installs (EREC/G100). We configure it — you never notice it.

Energy Performance Certificate — your home's energy rating. Not required for SEG. We still look at it at survey so you know where you stand if you later want other upgrades or grants.

Building control approval for electrical work. We notify via NAPIT (or NICEIC). Electrical Installation Certificate signed off by our NAPIT/NICEIC-registered electrician — legal requirement; the AI office logs it and sends it to you.

Home Insulation & Energy Systems — protects your deposit and gives an insurance-backed guarantee on workmanship. If we cease trading, HIES covers your install where membership applies.
