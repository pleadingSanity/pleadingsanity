Already Got Solar?

Already have solar? Stuck? We've got you.

Sorry we weren't here for you then. If you've got solar and you're stuck — left in the blue, don't know what to do — we can help. Bird cages (bird guards), upgrades, battery add-ons, EV chargers, inverter replacements, monitoring setup.

Here's how you finish it yourself — simple steps.

Most of these take 10 minutes and a phone. Try it yourself first — it's free.

G99 application
Big systems and batteries need the grid's permission first.
  • G99 is the form your DNO (grid operator) signs off — needed for systems over ~3.7kW single-phase, or any battery/EV that exports.
  • Apply with your MPAN (on your bill) and your system specs. Takes 5–15 working days.
  • You can't commission without it — your original installer should have done it.
Or call us — we handle G99/G98 applications for stuck systems.
SEG signup
You're exporting power for free if no one set this up.
  • Find your MPAN on any electric bill. Grab your MCS install certificate.
  • Pick an SEG supplier (list below) and ring them — they register your export.
  • Smart meter required so they read what you export. Pays 3–15p/kWh, more on smart tariffs.
Or call us — we do the SEG signup paperwork end to end.
Monitoring app setup
Every inverter has an app — you're missing free data.
  • Read the brand off the inverter front: Solis, GivEnergy, SolarEdge, Huawei, Sunsynk, Fox.
  • Download that brand's app. Connect the inverter to your home wifi (WPS button or wifi dongle).
  • We'll talk you through on WhatsApp — about 10 minutes.
Or call us — we set up monitoring remotely for £30.
Fault codes (red/amber light)
A flashing light usually means it lost the grid — not that it's dead.
  • Note the error code on the inverter screen.
  • Turn the AC isolator (switch near inverter) off, wait 60 seconds, turn back on. Check the DC isolator too.
  • If it re-flashes within the hour, ring the inverter brand's helpline — free, and usually under warranty.
Or call us — we diagnose fault codes and replace inverters.
Export limiter / G100
The grid makes you cap export on some installs — it's not your fault.
  • G100 is an export power limiter the DNO sometimes requires on big systems or batteries.
  • It caps what you send back to the grid. If your export reads 0 on a sunny day, it may be limiting.
  • We check whether it's required, and if allowed, upgrade or remove it.
Or call us — we fit and upgrade G100 limiters.
EPC — NOT required for SEG
Myth: you need an EPC. Fact: that was the old FIT, not SEG.
  • SEG (Smart Export Guarantee) does NOT require an EPC — that rule was for the old Feed-in Tariff, closed in 2019.
  • If a supplier tells you otherwise, pick another one.
  • You need an MCS certificate and a smart meter — that's it.
Or call us — we'll get you registered with the right supplier.
