More Than Clothes

I didn't start a clothing line to sell hoodies. I started it because so many of us carry things we can't say out loud. A piece of clothing can say it for you.

When you wear the Crying Brain, you're carrying the movement forward. You're telling someone on the bus, in the queue, at the school gate: I've been through it, and I'm still here. Sometimes that's all a person needs to see to feel less alone.

A Pleading Sanity-branded Reel from May 2025 describes clothing as a story stitched into fabric, carrying the message of turning pain into purpose. It captures the idea behind these designs: resilience in the seams, strength in the mind, and a way to start a conversation without explaining everything you've lived through.

The shop is optional. Wearing a logo is not treatment, and buying something is never a condition of joining the sanctuary. The free tools, games, journal and support pages are here whether you shop or not.

  • PS-H01: Crying Brain centred on the upper back, 220–240 mm wide. No P.S. beneath it. One left-chest P.S., 40–50 mm. Tonal matte-black PLEADING SANITY at the lowest rear ribbed hem. Width, letter height and seam offset wait for an approved 1:1 proof.
  • PS-J01: one P.S. on the wearer’s left thigh only, 50–65 mm. Right thigh, calves and the rear stay clean.
  • Leggings left-hip P.S. 60–70 mm. Mask-side P.S. 25–35 mm. Bra left-chest P.S. 30–40 mm. Women’s front waistband and bra underbust stay clean. Rear waistband wordmark only.
  • Signature-set sampling cloth route A: 420–450 gsm French terry. Organic status is unconfirmed. Do not claim organic. Quote the lighter stretch cloth separately. It must not replace route A without written approval.
  • PS-M01: lower panel sewn inside the hood; removable eye mesh on four fabric-covered neodymium magnetic points. Low-bulk stowage is a prototype target, not a zero-bulk, medical, PPE or protective result. The full-head mask is a separate experiment and is not PS-M01.
  • Pictures are design previews, including AI-generated campaign imagery. They are not print masters, stock, or proof of squat-proof, organic or performance claims. Written sizes control.
  • Final patterns, size grading, original artwork masters, colour swatches and a signed physical sample are still required before bulk manufacture.

Before you order or make a sample

Can I buy the new collection now?

The current collection is in development. These images are design previews, not a stock listing. Ask Shane about availability before paying. Pricing, delivery and returns must be confirmed for the item you choose.

How can a manufacturer help?

Start with the v1.1 factory pack above and say which styles you can make. Quote sample development and revisions, fabric, print and hardware, minimum quantities per style and colour, unit prices, lead times, delivery, taxes and exclusions separately. Flag the integrated mask before starting.

FOB figures are discussion targets, not agreed prices. Materials, placements and construction need Shane’s written approval before any substitution. An enquiry does not authorise production.

Could we discuss a partnership?

Proposals for sample development, manufacturing, stockists and clearly labelled sponsorship are welcome. Say what you can offer, what it costs and what you need in return. Any discount, credit, publicity or exclusivity must be agreed in writing. Sending a proposal does not grant ownership or access to community information.

Do I have to shop to use the sanctuary?

No. Arron, the journal, games, community feed and crisis support stay free. Clothing is optional. Private journals and personal information are never for sale.
