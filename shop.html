💙 More Than Clothes

I didn't start a clothing line to sell hoodies. I started it because so many of us carry things we can't say out loud. A piece of clothing can say it for you.

When you wear the Crying Brain, you're carrying the movement forward. You're telling someone on the bus, in the queue, at the school gate: I've been through it, and I'm still here. Sometimes that's all a person needs to see to feel less alone.

A Pleading Sanity-branded Reel from May 2025 describes clothing as a story stitched into fabric, carrying the message of turning pain into purpose. It captures the idea behind these designs: resilience in the seams, strength in the mind, and a way to start a conversation without explaining everything you've lived through.

The shop is optional. Wearing a logo is not treatment, and buying something is never a condition of joining the sanctuary. The free tools, games, journal and support pages are here whether you shop or not.

  • Every piece has meaning. No filler, no fakery. The tears are the pain. The glow is what we turn it into.
  • Net proceeds support the movement. Production, delivery, fees, returns and applicable taxes come before the money available for the free tools, games and support.
  • The house stays free. Talking to Arron, the journal, the breathing and the tools cost nothing. Buying is never the price of getting in.

Make it properly

A clear brief for the factory

Start with the 12-page v1.0 FINAL sampling pack. It includes the current monogram widths, mask technology, women’s specifications, artwork register and sample approval sequence.

Download factory sampling pack · v1.0 FINAL (PDF)Download editable design brief
  • All widths are in mm: hoodie chest 40–50; jogger left thigh 50–65; leggings left hip 60–70; mask side 25–35; bra left chest 30–40. One P.S. on the left thigh of each jogger only. Written sizes control; imagery is a design reference.
  • Women’s front waistband and bra underbust band stay clean. Small wordmark on the rear waistband only.
  • The grillz are a flexible printed graphic. The lower mask stays sewn to the hood; the eye mesh is removable; both stow clear of the face.
  • Use the written placement specification and approved artwork, not traced campaign images. Final patterns, size grading, artwork files, colour swatches and physical samples still need approval before bulk manufacture.

Before you order or make a sample

Can I buy the new collection now?

The current collection is in development. These images are design previews, not a stock listing. Ask Shane about availability before paying; final pricing, delivery and returns information must be confirmed for the item you choose.

How can a manufacturer help?

Start with the factory pack above and tell us which styles you can make. Quote sample development and revisions, fabric, print and hardware options, minimum quantities per style and colour, unit prices, lead times, delivery, taxes and exclusions separately. Flag construction concerns, especially the integrated mask, before starting.

FOB figures are discussion targets, not agreed prices. Materials, branding placements and construction need Shane’s written approval before any substitution. An enquiry does not authorise production or commit to an order.

Could we discuss a partnership?

We welcome proposals for sample development, manufacturing, stockists and clearly labelled sponsorship. Explain what you can offer, what it costs and what you would need in return. Any discount, credit, payment schedule, publicity or exclusivity must be agreed in writing. Sending a proposal does not grant ownership or access to community information.

Do I have to shop to use the sanctuary?

No. Arron, the journal, games, community feed and crisis support stay free. Clothing is optional, and private journals and personal information are never for sale.
