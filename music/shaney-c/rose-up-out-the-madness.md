# Rose Up Out the Madness (v2 working draft)

Artist: Shaney C (Shane Cooper)
Style: northern bassline / speed garage, around 138 BPM
Status: working draft for Shane's review. Built from Shane's own performed lines on the 15 Nov 2026 voice call, tightened for punch and rhyme. Shane approves every line before anything is recorded or released.
Originals only: no samples, no other artists' vocals or lyrics. The earlier "I'm blue" line was dropped because it echoed an existing song.
