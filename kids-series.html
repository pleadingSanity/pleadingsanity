Pleading Sanity original series · Ages 3–9

Keep Kids Sane: The Cartoon Series

Five friends discover how the world works, how feelings work, and how to help each other. Funny adventures, real science, gentle breathing and no pressure to be perfect.

Series development hub: Episodes are being developed. No finished video or YouTube upload is claimed yet.

Meet the friends

Ollie the Owl

The curious question-asker. Ollie checks facts and reminds everyone that not knowing yet is okay.

Zara the Squirrel

Inventive and full of energy. Zara turns mistakes into experiments.

Max

Brave, playful and sometimes impatient. Max learns that courage includes asking for help.

Mia

A thoughtful problem-solver who listens closely and makes room for everyone's feelings.

Blinky the Robot

A friendly little robot who learns about human emotions through questions and silly misunderstandings.

Episode plans

Pilot · Script concept

The Big Breath Balloon

When a science balloon won't lift off, the friends discover how air works and practise slow, comfortable breaths while they try again.

Science: air takes up space. Life skill: slowing down.

Episode 2 · Planned

Zara's Brilliant Mistake

Zara's invention tumbles apart. The team tests one change at a time and discovers why mistakes can teach us.

Science: predictions and testing. Life skill: resilience.

Episode 3 · Planned

Blinky's Feelings Decoder

Blinky tries to solve everyone's feelings with a calculator. Mia shows him why listening comes first.

Science: signals and senses. Life skill: empathy.

Read the pilot story outline

Ollie introduces the Great Sky Festival. Zara builds a balloon, Max wants to launch immediately, and Blinky counts down too soon. It falls flat. Mia suggests they stop, notice their breathing, and inspect the balloon. Ollie explains that air fills space. They fix a small leak, try again, and celebrate teamwork rather than winning. Everyone joins a gentle closing song about curiosity, kindness and taking things one step at a time.
