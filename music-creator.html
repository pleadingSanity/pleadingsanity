07 / YOUR NEXT STEP

Ready for YouTube?

  1. Download your voice take, mix and finished video. Watch the whole video and check every caption.
  2. Open YouTube or YouTube Studio, choose Create → Upload, and select the video. Start with an unlisted upload.
  3. Paste your title and description, add all required music credits, choose the correct audience setting and run YouTube’s checks. Resolve any copyright claim before publishing.
  4. For monetisation, open YouTube Studio → Earn. Enable 2-Step Verification and follow the eligibility and AdSense steps shown for your channel.

No app can promise ad revenue, approval or zero copyright claims. Shorts over one minute with an active Content ID claim are blocked until it is resolved. Mental-health topics and explicit lyrics may affect advertiser suitability.
