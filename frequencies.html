How to use these tones

There is no right way to do this. But a few simple things help.

  1. Find a quiet spot. Sit or lie down. Phone on silent if you can.
  2. Headphones help. They keep the room out. Speakers are fine too.
  3. Start with the volume low. Lower than you think. You should never strain to hear it, and it should never hurt.
  4. Give it 5 to 15 minutes. That is plenty. Longer is not better.
  5. Breathe slowly. In through the nose for four. Out through the mouth for six. Let the tone be something to rest your mind on.
  6. Stop whenever you like. If you feel dizzy, uneasy, or get a headache, stop. That is you listening to yourself, and that matters more than any tone.

What each tone is said to mean

Many of these numbers come from old sound traditions, often called the solfeggio tones. People have found meaning in them for a long time. Below is what they are traditionally linked with — told plainly, so you can decide for yourself.

174 Hz — ground

The lowest tone here. People often link it with feeling grounded, safe and settled in the body. Try it when your head feels busy and you want to come back down.

285 Hz — renewal

Traditionally tied to restoring and renewing — a sense of starting again gently. A warm tone to sit with after a hard day.

432 Hz — calm

Some people call 432 Hz a more “natural” tuning and say music tuned to it feels softer and easier on the ear. It is often used for calm and for winding down before sleep.

528 Hz — love and repair

Often called the “love frequency”. People associate it with change, repair and opening the heart. A clear, bright middle tone that many find easy to focus on.

741 Hz — clearing

Linked with clearing the mind and finding your voice — saying what you really mean. Try it before you write, or when your thoughts feel tangled.

963 Hz — connection

The highest tone here. Traditionally tied to a sense of oneness and connection to something bigger than yourself. It is high-pitched, so keep the volume right down.

Two more you may hear about

These two are not on this player yet, but people often ask about them.

  • 639 Hz — linked with relationships, connection and understanding others. Some use it when they feel cut off or lonely.
  • 852 Hz — linked with intuition, inner clarity and quieting the overthinking mind.

The honest truth

This is a calming practice, not a treatment. The science on specific frequencies is limited. There is no good evidence that one number heals something another number cannot. These tones are not a cure for anything.

What does help — and this part is well known — is slowing down, breathing gently and giving yourself a quiet moment. The tone is a doorway to that. If a certain tone means something to you, that meaning is real and it is yours. We respect it.
