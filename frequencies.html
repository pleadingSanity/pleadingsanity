Healing Hz

Ten soft tones. One at a time.

The number you pick is really in the sound. Under it: wind, water, and a slow chord. Not a cure. If you are new, test 174 or 432 for eight seconds, then sit for five minutes.

174 Hz
Ground
The lowest tone. People sit with it when the head is busy and they want to come back down. Heavy, quiet, steady.
New listener: 5 minutes, once a day. A good place to start.
285 Hz
Warm
A little warmer. People link it with starting again gently after a hard day. Still quiet.
New listener: 5 minutes, once a day, after 174 feels easy.
432 Hz
Calm
A calm tuning. Some people find it softer than ordinary concert pitch. Easy to sit with before sleep.
New listener: 5 minutes, once a day. The other good place to start.
528 Hz
Clear
A clear middle tone. People call it the love frequency and sit with it when they want to feel a bit more open. It does not repair the body.
New listener: 5 minutes, once a day.
741 Hz
Clearing
Brighter. People sit with it before they write, or when thoughts are tangled and they want a cleaner line. Keep it soft.
New listener: 3 to 5 minutes, and not on the first day.
963 Hz
High
The highest. People link it with a sense of being part of something larger. It is a thin tone. Volume right down, or it will annoy you.
New listener: 3 minutes. Leave this until the rest feel kind.

How long, if you are new

One tone a day. Do not play the whole set in one sitting. Longer is not better. The slider already stays low. Leave it there.

WhenWhich toneHow long
First week174 Hz or 432 Hz5 minutes, once a day
Second weekOne tone that felt kind10 minutes, once a day
After thatOne toneUp to 15 minutes, once a day
Bright tones741, 852, 963 Hz3 to 5 minutes, not on day one
  1. Same time each day helps more than a longer session.
  2. Sit or lie down. Headphones if you want the room quieter. Speakers are fine.
  3. Breathe slowly. In through the nose for four. Out for six. The tone is something to rest on.
  4. A second sit only if the first one left you settled. Not to catch up.
  5. Stop if you feel dizzy, uneasy, or get a headache. That matters more than the minutes.

What people say each tone is for

These meanings were written down in the 1990s, not in old chant books. They are a way people have chosen to sit with a number. They are not a medical effect. The picture on each tone is only a picture. The tone is the practice.

  • 174 — come back down when the head is busy.
  • 285 — a warmer start after a hard day.
  • 396 — sit with a weight you are ready to put down.
  • 417 — a small change, when something feels stuck.
  • 432 — calm. The other gentle place to begin.
  • 528 — a clear middle tone. People call it love. It does not repair anything.
  • 639 — company when you feel cut off from someone.
  • 741 — a cleaner line before you write.
  • 852 — a quieter mind. Easy to overdo.
  • 963 — the thin high tone. Volume down, and only for a few minutes.

The honest truth

This is a calming practice, not a treatment. The science on specific frequencies is limited. There is no good evidence that one number heals something another number cannot. These tones are not a cure for anything.

The hertz you choose is actually playing. Under it, this page makes a soft earth bed in the browser: wind, water, and a slow chord dropped into a gentler octave. That bed is not a recording of a real forest, and it is not a song from a record. The Test button plays eight seconds so you can hear the truth of it before you sit.

What does help — and this part is well known — is slowing down, breathing gently and giving yourself a quiet moment. The tone is a doorway to that. If a certain tone means something to you, that meaning is real and it is yours. We respect it.

If you are struggling right now, a tone is not enough on its own, and you deserve more than that. Read the house truth or see how to use this house.
