02 · Technical buyer's guide

Why one kit costs more than another

Solar modules

PERC vs TOPCon vs HJT vs back-contact

PERC: often lower-cost legacy stock, with lower efficiency on comparable models. TOPCon: widely available N-type mainstream choice. HJT: can offer strong temperature behaviour but often costs more. Back-contact: high area efficiency and clean appearance, often a premium option.

Compare exact datasheets: Wp, efficiency, size, temperature coefficient, product warranty, degradation and price per Wp.

Inverters

String vs hybrid vs microinverters

String: straightforward and often lower upfront cost for unshaded arrays. Hybrid: battery integration, but check compatibility and backup capability. Microinverters: module-level conversion and monitoring, typically higher equipment and installation cost.

Check MPPT ranges, DC/AC sizing, phases, grid approval, monitoring and service support.

Batteries

Usable kWh matters more than headline kWh

Compare usable capacity, continuous and peak power, chemistry, cycle warranty, compatible inverter, temperature limits, commissioning and whether backup requires additional hardware.

Compare total installed £ per usable kWh, not just the price of the battery module.

Mounting & BOS

Cheap rails can make an expensive job

Roof type, wind loads, corrosion resistance, fixing method, ballast, fire and structural requirements determine the system. Include clamps, hooks, cables, isolators, protection and delivery in every bill of materials.

Use manufacturer-approved designs and qualified structural/electrical review.

04 · Larger businesses

From one roof to a repeatable procurement operation

Standardise the bill of materials

Approved part numbers, alternates and documented engineering checks reduce substitutions and rework.

Compare total landed cost

Track ex-VAT equipment cost, freight, handling, credit, labour, warranty risk and time-to-site — not just headline module price.

Run controlled supplier trials

Request multiple comparable RFQs, confirm technical support and monitor on-time deliveries before negotiating volume terms.

Keep a human sign-off

Buyers, designers, qualified electricians and compliance teams approve the final specification. AI can help organise information, not certify a system.
