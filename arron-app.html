          Your mind, your data

          Everything lives on this device first. When online, it syncs to Pleading Sanity's own database: linked only to your code as a guest, or to your account if you are signed in. Replies come from GPT, Claude or Gemini through Netlify's AI Gateway, or from Grok through xAI: if one is down, the next steps in. If all are down, you get the real help lines instead (see Privacy). Messages are always sent to the provider that answers, even when your chat is kept on this device. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, never sold.
