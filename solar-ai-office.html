The humans on your roof

What humans do

The AI doesn't touch a roof, a cable or a certificate. These five jobs are done by real people — and only real people can do them.

  • 1
    Survey
    A real surveyor measures your roof, checks structure and shading, and confirms the quote.
  • 2
    Install
    The same crew fits your panels, inverter and battery — typically 1–2 days, scaffold cleared same day.
  • 3
    Electrical sign-off
    A registered electrician tests and certifies the installation (Part P / EICR).
  • 4
    MCS / NAPIT certificates
    Issued and signed by our MCS / NAPIT-registered installer — the AI only assembles the paperwork.
  • 5
    Final approval
    You sign off the finished job. A human, not a bot, walks you round the array and the monitoring app.
