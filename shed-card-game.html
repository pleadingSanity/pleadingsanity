HOW TO PLAY

  • Beat the top discard: played cards must be equal or higher rank (2 low, Ace high). Same rank is allowed — play doubles / triples.
  • 2 is wild — play it on anything and the pile resets (next player can play anything).
  • 10 burns the whole pile (playable on anything). Playing four of the same rank in one turn also burns the pile.
  • Can't (or won't) play? Tap Pick up pile — the whole discard pile goes into your hand.
  • After playing, draw back up to 3 hand cards until the deck runs out.
  • No hand left? Play your 3 face-up table cards. Then flip your face-down blind cards one at a time — if a blind card can't be played, you pick up the pile.
  • First to shed every card wins. Last player holding cards is the Shed (aka Shithead).
