Frequently asked questions

Can I broadcast directly on Pleading Sanity right now?

No. Native direct broadcasting is still being built. Right now you bring a public YouTube or Twitch stream, and we help you preview it and share it with the community.

Do you make automatic clips of my stream?

Not yet. Automatic AI highlight clips and downloadable clips are in development and are not available today.

Do creators get paid?

A Founding Creator offer (90% of qualifying income for the first 1,000 eligible creators) is planned, but payouts are not live and no payment is promised. No income generated means no payout.

What do I need to bring a stream?

A public YouTube or Twitch URL. Anyone can preview it; sharing to the community feed requires sign-in. Playback depends on the original platform and your stream settings.

Is my data sold?

No. Pleading Sanity does not sell your data. See our Privacy page for details.
