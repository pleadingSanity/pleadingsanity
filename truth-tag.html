Why truth tags matter here

Every post on the Sanctuary carries a truth tag, so readers know what kind of truth they're reading:

  • 🔬 Evidence — something that can be checked: research, data, verifiable facts.
  • 💙 Experience — personal lived truth. Real and valid, but one person's story.
  • 🌌 Philosophy — beliefs, values, opinions and interpretations.

None is better than another. Each deserves respect — they just do different jobs. Mixing them up is how myths spread and how people end up feeling unheard.
