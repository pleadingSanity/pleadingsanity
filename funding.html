Funding Pleading Sanity. Free forever.

The sanctuary is free. It will always be free. You can use it without buying anything, signing in, or paying anyone. This page explains what keeping it free costs, where money could come from, and what is — and is not — on offer.

No paywall. No venture capital. No ads. No data sales. Talk, journal, games, the feed and Arron are free. We take no venture capital and never will. The sanctuary offers no shares or financial return.

How we report it

Funding is transparent by default. If the commercial projects earn a profit, we will publish the share allocated to sanctuary upkeep and mental health charities. Each year we will report actual amounts, recipients and costs, including years when nothing was distributed. Formal audited accounts and pound-by-pound amounts are not published yet; no figures are estimated on this page. Once real books exist, a dated funding table will be added here and linked from the public changelog.

Money received is not the same as money available to reinvest. Supporters are not publicly named without permission. Personal thank-yous and social contact are optional and arranged privately with Shane.
