# Keep Kids Sane — The Quiet Meteor

**Status:** DRAFT · Ages 3–9 · Not filmed · Not uploaded · £0 · No pictures generated
**This is not Episode 2.** Episode 2 on `main` is already *Zara’s Brilliant Mistake* in `docs/kids-animation-ready.md`. Do not renumber that file. Do not replace it.
**Where this sits:** A later story in the same series. Same sky, same friends. The Crying Brain is the sky guide, not a new owner of the show.
**Learning:** When everything is loud at once, pick one gentle thing. You can pause. You can tell a grown-up.
**Safety:** No strobe. No crash sounds. No rocks hitting anyone. The “meteors” are slow glowing pebbles of light. A parent can pause at any time. Breathing is optional.

## Shared rules, carried over

- Sky `#000103`, soft stars, faint cyan and pink. Never busy.
- Cuts stay slow. Subtitles always on, large frost text.
- Cast: Ollie the Owl, Zara the Squirrel, Max, Mia, Blinky the Robot.
- The Crying Brain: the cosmic mark, cyan edge, pink glow, a calm voice. It does not cry in distress in front of the children. The light in its eyes is gentle, like a night light.
- Music: the same soft guitar and pad. No 808. No siren.

## Timed screenplay

**Runtime target:** 3:40

**0:00–0:20** Wide night sky. The friends sit on a soft hill. One pale pebble of light drifts past, slow.
**Ollie (warm):** The sky is sending little lights tonight.
**Mia:** We can watch one.

**0:20–0:50** More pebbles. Still slow, still quiet. Zara’s ears twitch. Max’s cape lifts. Blinky’s light flickers, then he covers it with a soft hand so it will not flash.
**Zara (smaller voice):** There are a lot now.
**Max:** I can’t tell which one to look at.
**Blinky (quiet beep):** Too many inputs.

**0:50–1:20** The lights bunch together. They do not hit the ground. They hang in the air like lanterns that forgot to take turns. The picture does not strobe. It just feels full.
**On screen, large:** You can pause.
**Mia:** If this feels like a lot, we stop. The hill will wait.

**1:20–1:50** The Crying Brain appears in the upper sky, small at first, then near enough to see. No sudden zoom. Cyan rim, pink glow.
**Crying Brain (slow):** The sky got crowded. We do not have to meet every light. Feet on the hill. One light. Then the next.

**1:50–2:30** Each friend chooses one pebble and names one ordinary thing.
**Ollie:** One owl feather on my wing.
**Zara:** One warm tail.
**Max:** One shoe on the grass.
**Mia:** One lantern, turned down.
**Blinky:** One soft beep. Only one.
The other lights drift aside. They do not explode. They make room.

**2:30–3:05** The bunch becomes a slow path of single lights. The friends walk under them, one step each.
**Crying Brain:** Loud does not mean danger. Crowded does not mean you failed. One thing is enough.
**Ollie:** And if one thing is still too many, we tell a grown-up and we rest.

**3:05–3:40** Wide hill again. A few lights left, far apart. Friends sitting. The Brain stays in the sky, small, not looming.
**All (soft):** One light. Then the next.
**Closing song:** The sky can wait. My feet are here. One small light, and a friend is near.

## Voice

- Crying Brain: low, warm, never urgent. No whisper that sounds like a secret.
- Blinky: one beep, then words. He turns his own light down.
- Nobody shouts over the sky. If a line needs to be louder, cut the other sound instead.

## Sound

- A soft chime when one light is chosen. Not a whoosh, not a bang.
- When the sky is crowded, the pad thins out. Silence is allowed.
- No binaural pulses. No healing-frequency claim. This is a story, not a treatment.

## On-screen words

## Parent note

Some children find a busy sky too much. This episode is the practice for that, and it is also allowed to be switched off. The point is not to push through a storm. The point is that one gentle thing is allowed, and a grown-up can take over. Do not tell a child that noticing a shoe will fix panic. It is a first small step, and stopping is a success too.
