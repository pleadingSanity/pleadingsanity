# Insane Radio — DAB and community broadcast plan

## Station identity
- Name: Insane Radio
- Mission: Independent music radio — timeless hip hop and R&B, no commercial influence, free for all listeners, mental health signposting.
- Programme: Curated music + founder announcements. No paid content. No politics. No religious agenda.

## Phases
1. Online stream on pleadingsanity.co.uk — now.
2. Community DAB application — Leicestershire first.
3. FM community licence — long-term, research ready.

## Technical
- Stream: 128kbps MP3 + AAC-HE, DAB-ready.
- RDS name: INSANE
- Scrolling text: Insane Radio — Music That Speaks

## Funding
Zero licence fee. Volunteer-run. No commercial advertising revenue. Community grants only if needed.

## Ofcom
Community radio licence template and eligibility checklist to be completed when application window opens.
