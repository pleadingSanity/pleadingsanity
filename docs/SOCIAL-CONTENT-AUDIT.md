## How to conduct the real audit
1. **Authorised connection:** Use only the account owner's explicit OAuth or social analytics connector (e.g. a supported social analytics app), or platform-provided exports the founder uploads. Check which years, post types, comments and insights are available. No scraping private accounts or bypassing API limitations.
2. **Coverage ledger:** For each platform log post count reviewed, earliest/latest timestamp, unsupported years, missing images/videos, and whether content was public/private. Distinguish "all accessible" from "all ever posted".
3. **Per-post record:** Platform, original link or export ID, timestamp, caption or transcript, media type, author, audience, theme, reuse permission, uncertainty, and useful ideas. Never infer the author's medical status, politics, beliefs or biography from algorithmic guesses.
4. **Topic synthesis:** Separate demonstrated history, first-person self-report, family oral history, proposals/visions, achievements proven with records, and creative content. Show the evidence behind every summary; do not write founder biography from third-party likes, reposts or comments alone.
5. **Founder review:** Present the draft narrative and every suggested public excerpt or media reuse for human approval. Exclude children, unrelated individuals, sensitive health details, third-party contact information, deleted/private posts and potentially identifying material unless the owner explicitly approves and legal rights permit.
6. **Publication:** On approval, add short, relevant details only where they serve the visitor: `about.html` (accurate founder voice/heritage), `movement.html` (approved mission/values), `creator.html` (creator journey), `kids-series.html` (approved educational ideas), `docs/creative-content-pack.md` (drafts), `AI_HANDOFF.md` (high-level approved findings). Avoid filling every footer with social links.
7. **Privacy:** Raw social exports, login tokens, comments by other users and sensitive drafts MUST NOT be committed to this public repository. Local notes in `/builder.html` stay in that browser profile and are not a secure vault. Use redacted summaries in GitHub only.
8. **Cost:** Additional spend authorised = £0. Free-tier or startup options must not silently activate paid usage.

### Personal-history export — the appropriate route to wider coverage

1. Open Facebook or Instagram settings, then **Accounts Centre → Your information and permissions → Export your information** (some versions say Download your information).
2. Choose **Create export** or **Download or transfer information**, select the owned Facebook/Instagram profile(s), and choose **Export to device / Download to device**. Repeat per profile if the interface requires it.
3. Select the available content categories: posts, comments/replies, photos/videos, Reels, Stories and relevant group activity. For the whole personal archive choose the available-all-information option, but keep the master private; direct messages, security logs and contacts are unnecessary for this editorial review.
4. Choose **All time**, **JSON**, desired media quality and notification address, then **Start export / Create files**. Complete any password/2FA confirmation only inside Meta. Labels and available categories vary; follow Meta's current Help Centre if different.
5. Download every archive part when notified. Keep originals privately. Share only a selected/redacted posts-and-comments subset for this audit; exclude credentials, messages, private third-party information and children's details. Do not upload it to GitHub, the website or a public issue.
6. For a missing thread, use your own Facebook **Activity log → Comments** and date filter around the remembered period, then save your own comment/permalink/context privately. Export coverage can omit deleted/unavailable content and other people’s full threads; never promise recovery of every historical item.
7. Keep a separate private ledger of source file, timestamp/timezone, permalink/ID, authorship, original audience, excerpt, consent, evidence status and intended public use. Reconcile all archive parts, duplicates and missing media before calling coverage complete.
