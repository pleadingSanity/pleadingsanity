# Brand, Intellectual Property and Security Protection Plan

**Scope:** Pleading Sanity, pleadingsanity.co.uk, original crying-brain artwork, source code, written material, user contributions and the founder's creative archive. This is an operational plan, not a legal determination.

## What can be protected
- Preserve dated originals and Git commit history as provenance evidence. Back up files offsite.
- Audit original artwork, names, copy, video and audio for copyright ownership and third-party licences before making rights claims.
- Consider UK trademark clearance and registration for **Pleading Sanity** and appropriate logos/classes. Check existing marks first and seek qualified UK IP advice if needed.
- Publish a clear, accurate copyright and permitted-use policy. Do not imply that all concepts, general ideas, AI outputs or publicly available patterns are exclusively owned.
- Document any third-party software licences. Keep licence notices and comply with open-source obligations.

## Similarity and infringement monitoring
1. Record approved brand identifiers, canonical domain, known official social profiles and authorised licensees.
2. Periodically search for suspiciously similar domains, copied text and reused original artwork.
3. Review evidence manually: screenshots, timestamps, URLs, archived pages and comparison of genuinely original elements.
4. Distinguish coincidence, lawful inspiration, licensed use, parody, interoperability and potential infringement.
5. Where justified, use registrar/host abuse reports, trademark procedures, copyright takedown notices or legal advice.
6. **Never** inject warnings into third-party sites, accuse a site automatically, or demand money to remove an unsupported warning.
7. Licensing or brand partnerships must be voluntary and documented; do not condition removal of false allegations on payment.

## Public-site authenticity
- Keep canonical tags, official contact and domain references consistent.
- Consider a public 'Official Pleading Sanity' verification page listing only founder-approved properties.
- A notice on **our own** site may warn visitors that unauthorised imitations exist if backed by evidence. Do not publish unsupported allegations.
- Configure domain renewal, DNS account recovery, MFA and registrar lock where supported.

## Security priorities
- Inventory all deployed functions, public assets, redirects and secrets exposure.
- Protect GitHub with MFA, least-privilege access, protected branches, PR review and secret scanning.
- Review Firebase Authentication, Firestore/Storage rules and App Check suitability; verify actual permissions in tests.
- Audit Netlify functions for authz, rate limiting, input validation, secret handling, abuse and cost ceilings.
- Add browser security headers/CSP incrementally with testing; avoid breaking Firebase, social login or embedded content.
- Audit third-party dependencies and licences; schedule automated dependency and security checks.
- Maintain secure backups, incident response and recovery runbooks. No passwords, service-account keys or private family archives in public Git.

## Founder prompt and family archive
- Export conversations and prompts using the relevant providers' official export mechanisms, where available.
- Label sources, dates, AI-assisted content and access permissions; keep personal/family details private by default.
- Preserve a human-readable copy and at least two independently controlled backups.
- Do not assert that an AI provider must give us access to its internal training data, or that past prompts prove ownership of model capabilities.
- Preserve an offline/read-only version of the story so future access does not depend on paid AI services.

## Implementation order
1. Verify repo licence and provenance; establish brand policy with legal review.
2. Run security baseline and prioritise exploitable findings.
3. Add evidence-based monitoring and official-domain verification.
4. Build encrypted private archive and succession instructions with owner approval.
5. Re-test after each security change; keep changes scoped and reversible.

**Status:** Planning only. No third-party scanning, infringement claims, trademark registration, vulnerability remediation or private archive deployment has yet been performed.
