# 3D print models matched to the first Pleading Sanity clothing line (15 Nov 2026)

Original designs only, taken from the clothing on [shop.html](../shop.html): the signature black set (PS-H01 Crying Brain hoodie, PS-J01 joggers), the full-zip and quarter-zip (PS-Z01, PS-Q01), the graphic capsule (PS-T01 half-brain tee) and the women's set (PS-L01 leggings, PS-B01 sports bra, black and charcoal). Motifs used: the serif **P.S.** monogram, the **Crying Brain** with its two tear drips, the **half brain** with one drip and sparkles, and the **PLEADING SANITY** wordmark. Taglines for cards and inserts: "Rise From Madness", "Strength / Struggle / Hope", "A brain that cries is a brain that's still fighting."

**About "kids" and "girls" versions.** There is no children's clothing line in the repo yet (kids.html is the Keep Kids Sane cartoon series, not clothing). So every model here is a **14+ decorative accessory** (keyring, bag charm, zip charm or display piece) that matches the adult clothing, with no child imagery and no toy marketing. The "girls' and women's" set is designed for the women's range and is fine for teens 14+. If a true children's line and child-aimed prints are wanted later, they are toys and need the full toy route in [3D-PRINT-COMPLIANCE-UK.md](3D-PRINT-COMPLIANCE-UK.md) first.

All prints: Flashforge AD5X, SUNLU PLA+ 1.75 mm black with FLASHFORGE Rapid PLA 1.75 mm white (two-colour by filament swap at a layer height, or the AD5X multi-colour), 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 20% infill unless noted. Keyring hole 4 mm with at least 2.5 mm of material around it. Times are estimates: replace with the slicer's numbers in `print_hours` and `weight_g`.

All are **solid and flat (2D with raised relief)** on purpose: flat, low play value accessories are not toys; 3D figures and flexi keyrings are. [Guidance Document No 20](https://www.assogiocattoli.eu/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/FinalGuidance_decorative-products_productsforcollectors.pdf)

### 5. Crying Brain desk sculpture — `crying-brain-sculpture`

- Size: 55 x 50 x 70 mm (W x D x H). Drips at least 6 mm thick.
- Solid, static display piece, 14+. 15% gyroid infill, tree supports under the brain. Print time about 4-5 h. About 38 g. Price £8. Not a keyring.

## Before any of these go live
1. Design the actual print file (Tinkercad, Fusion or Blender) from the brief above; the catalogue licence says "concept only" until then. Change `licence` to "Original design, Pleading Sanity" when the file is yours.
2. Print, check for sharp edges and weak hole walls, drop-test once from 1 m, measure.
3. Photograph the real print, upload it on creator-prints.html, untick mockup, keep batch B001 (or the real batch), set Live.
4. Parcel insert with maker name, postal address and batch, plus the warnings.

Mockups (not photos of real prints) are in `images/3d-prints/` with the item ids above. The P.S. monogram, wordmark and zip charm mockups are vector design renders; the others are AI-generated product mockups.
