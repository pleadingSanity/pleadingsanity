No text, no logos, no watermarks, no crying-brain overuse.

"Painterly volumetric deep-space scene, near-black #0b0f19 background, teal #00fff0 and #38bdf8 light beams, soft amber #f59e0b rim light, a single bright amber accent star, no text, no logos, no watermarks, 16:9, sanctuary atmosphere"
