# Pleading Sanity - Strategic Partnership Ecosystem
## Building Forever: Alliance Architecture for Global Mental Health Impact

---

## 🤝 Partnership Philosophy

Pleading Sanity's partnership strategy is built on **mutual benefit and shared values**, not just business transactions. Every partnership must:

1. **Enhance User Value**: Directly improve mental health outcomes for our community
2. **Preserve Authenticity**: Maintain our survivor-led, unfiltered approach
3. **Protect Privacy**: Never compromise user data or privacy for commercial gain
4. **Advance Mission**: Contribute to global mental health awareness and support
5. **Enable Scale**: Help us reach more people who need support

**We partner with organizations that believe mental health support should be accessible, authentic, and community-driven.**

This partnership ecosystem ensures that Pleading Sanity can reach every person who needs mental health support, regardless of where they are in their journey or what systems they're already connected to. 

**We're not trying to replace existing mental health infrastructure - we're trying to enhance it, connect it, and make it more effective for the people it serves.**

The partnerships serve the community, not the other way around.
