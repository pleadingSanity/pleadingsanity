# Pleading Sanity — One Page Overview

## What we are
A free mental health sanctuary built by Shane Cooper from lived experience with bipolar disorder.

## What we offer
- Arron, a gentle AI companion
- Digital journals and self-understanding tools
- Healing games and calming frequencies
- A community feed
- A creator hub (any revenue share is a proposal until terms are approved)
- Crisis links on /crisis.html
- An installable app that works offline

## How we operate
- Privacy first: local device storage and a Forget Me wipe
- Talk, journal, games, feed and Arron are free
- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript

## What makes us different
We build to heal, not to grow.

## The vision
Not the biggest mental health platform. The kindest.
