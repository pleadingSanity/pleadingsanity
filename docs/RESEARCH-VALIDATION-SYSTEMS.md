## Building Forever: Evidence-Based Foundation for Global Mental Health Impact

---

## 🔬 Research Philosophy

Pleading Sanity's research approach is grounded in **community-participatory action research** that:

1. **Centers Lived Experience**: Survivors and community members are co-researchers, not subjects
2. **Generates Actionable Evidence**: Research directly improves platform effectiveness and user outcomes
3. **Maintains Ethical Standards**: Rigorous protection of privacy, consent, and community autonomy
4. **Advances Open Science**: Findings are shared to benefit the global mental health community
5. **Validates Impact**: Continuous measurement ensures we're actually helping, not just assuming we are

**Our research validates that peer support, authentic community, and accessible technology can save lives and transform mental health outcomes.**

## 🔍 Research Quality & Ethics

### **Research Ethics Framework**

#### **Community-Centered Ethics:**
```
Survivor Leadership Principles
├── Research questions prioritized by community needs
├── Community members trained as co-researchers
├── Results interpreted through lived experience lens
├── Community benefits prioritized over academic publications
└── Community consent required for all research activities

Vulnerable Population Protection
├── Additional safeguards for youth, elderly, severe mental illness
├── Cultural competency requirements for all research staff
├── Trauma-informed research methodologies
├── Crisis intervention protocols during research participation
└── Long-term follow-up care & support provision
```

#### **Data Ethics & Privacy:**
```
Privacy by Design
├── Data minimization (Collect only what's necessary)
├── Purpose limitation (Use data only for stated research purposes)
├── Storage limitation (Delete data when no longer needed)
├── Transparency (Clear communication about data use)
└── User control (Participants can modify/withdraw consent)

Security & Confidentiality
├── End-to-end encryption for all research data
├── Anonymization & pseudonymization protocols
├── Secure multi-party computation for sensitive analyses
├── Regular security audits & penetration testing
└── Incident response & breach notification procedures
```

### **Research Quality Assurance**

#### **Methodological Rigor:**
```
Study Design Standards
├── Pre-registration of all studies (Open Science Framework)
├── Power analyses & sample size calculations
├── Control group selection & randomization procedures
├── Blinding protocols where applicable
└── Statistical analysis plan pre-specification

Reproducibility & Open Science
├── Code & data sharing (where privacy allows)
├── Replication studies encouraged & funded
├── Null results publication commitment
├── Meta-analysis & systematic review integration
└── Research protocol transparency & public availability
```

#### **Independent Oversight:**

- **Core Platform Effectiveness**: Randomized controlled trials of key platform features
