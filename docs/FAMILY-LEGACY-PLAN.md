# Family Legacy Preservation — Planning Guide

Pleading Sanity is both a public project and a potential record of its founder's work, beliefs, humour, creativity and care for family. This is a **planning document**, not a published memorial, a claim about anyone's health, or permission to expose family information.

## Preserve the authentic record
- Save dated original writing, project notes, artwork, photographs and recordings in their original formats.
- Preserve the founder's own words separately from AI-generated interpretations or rewrites.
- Record provenance: original date, creator, file source, consent and whether material was AI-assisted.
- Back up the GitHub repository, website assets and documentation to at least two independently controlled locations.
- Maintain a plain-language inventory so future family members can find important material without needing technical knowledge.

## Arron as a companion and guide
- Arron can explain the project's history and help visitors navigate material that the founder deliberately made available.
- Arron must clearly say it is an AI; it must not impersonate the founder or invent private memories, promises or opinions.
- Quotes, voice and likeness must be used only with appropriate consent and clear labels.
- Do not promise perpetual operation: maintain an exportable archive so the legacy remains accessible if AI services or hosting end.

## Family privacy and continuity
- Keep private letters, children's details, family photographs and personal recordings in a **private archive** by default, never in this public repository.
- Allow the founder to select what is public, family-only or private, with the ability to revise consent.
- Document domain renewals, hosting, GitHub/Firebase ownership and emergency account recovery **outside the repository**, in a secure password manager or legal estate plan.
- Consider an authorised successor and written instructions for maintaining or shutting down services. Do not put passwords or recovery codes in GitHub.
- Preserve an accessible, static read-only version of the founder's story even if Arron is unavailable.

## Future implementation — requires owner decisions
1. A public, factual `Our Story` timeline using approved source material.
2. An exportable family archive, with private storage and explicit access permissions.
3. Optional labelled original audio/video messages.
4. A read-only Arron archive mode with grounded citations to approved material and an explicit AI identity.
5. A recovery and maintenance playbook with cost ceilings.

**Do not publish private family content or enable AI voice impersonation automatically.** Confirm each item's intended audience and consent first.

Principles: Evolution, Not Erasure. Love Over Money. Truth Over Perfection.
