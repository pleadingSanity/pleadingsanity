- "Five songs. One voice. Back to the music that matters."
- "From the Bronx to Brixton. LA to Leicester. Hip Hop has no borders."

- "If this music speaks to you — tell someone. You're not alone."
- "Every artist you hear built their craft in the dark, just like you."
- "We don't do ads. We do this because music heals."
- "Insane Radio — both sides of the Atlantic. One frequency."

Place files in /audio/announcements/ when recorded.
