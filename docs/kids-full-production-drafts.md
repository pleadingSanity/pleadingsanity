# Keep Kids Sane — Full Pilot Production Drafts
**Status:** DRAFT · Ages 3–9 · Scripts + storyboard beats + voice notes · Not filmed · Not uploaded  
**Characters:** Ollie the Owl, Zara the Squirrel, Max, Mia, Blinky the Robot  
**House rule:** Funny, kind, real science, no pressure to be perfect. Evolution Not Erasure.  
**Safety:** Separate from adult community. No private child information. Parents stay in charge.

## Shared visual & voice notes
- Palette: `#000103` night sky, soft cyan `#00fff0`, gentle purple, frost text.
- Style: rounded shapes, big eyes, soft lighting, calm cosmic background.

- Zara: quick, excited, slightly higher pitch. Tool belt, messy tail.

- Mia: calm, clear, soft lantern light. Good at noticing feelings.

## Episode 1 — The Big Breath Balloon
**Learning:** Air takes up space. Slowing down helps. Teamwork.

### Full dialogue draft
**Ollie:** Welcome to the Great Sky Festival! Today we see whose balloon can float the highest.  
**Zara:** Mine’s ready! Look at the colours!  
**Max:** Launch it now! I want to see it go!  
**Blinky:** Counting down… three… two…  
**Mia:** Wait — let’s check it first.  
*(Balloon sags flat)*  
**Zara:** Oh no… it fell.  
**Max:** (disappointed, not angry) It didn’t even lift.  
**Mia:** Let’s stop and notice our breath. In… and out. Three soft breaths together.  
**Ollie:** Air fills the balloon. If there’s a little hole, the air sneaks out.  
**Zara:** (finding the leak) Here! A tiny hole!  
**Max:** We can fix that.  
*(They patch it, try again. Balloon rises gently)*  
**All:** It works!  
**Ollie:** The highest part was helping each other.  
**Closing song:** “One breath, one try, one friend at a time. The sky is big enough for everyone.”

### Storyboard beats (6 frames)
1. Festival banner, friends gathering, balloon half-built.  
2. Max leaning forward, Blinky counting, Zara holding the balloon.  
3. Balloon flat on the ground, soft disappointed faces.  
4. Mia holding lantern, group breathing together, soft glow.  
5. Close-up of small leak being patched, Ollie pointing.  
6. Balloon rising gently, friends smiling, not racing.

**YouTube title concept:** The Big Breath Balloon | Keep Kids Sane  
**Thumbnail:** Balloon rising with soft tears of joy (light, not crying), friends looking up.

## Episode 2 — Zara’s Brilliant Mistake
**Learning:** One change at a time. Mistakes teach. Resilience.

### Full dialogue draft
**Zara:** Watch my new stacker! It should make the tallest tower ever!  
**Max:** Do it fast!  
*(Tower tumbles)*  
**Zara:** (embarrassed) It broke.  
**Max:** I can fix it quick!  
**Mia:** Let’s try one change at a time.  
**Ollie:** What if the base is wider?  
*(They test. It holds better)*  
**Blinky:** Base wider… data good.  
**Zara:** Now lighter blocks?  
*(Works better)*  
**Mia:** See? The mistake showed us what to try next.  
**Blinky:** Mistake = data!  
**All:** (laugh)  
**Closing:** They high-five the learning, not a perfect score.

### Storyboard beats
1. Zara presenting the invention with pride.  
2. Tower falling, soft “oh” faces.  
3. Group writing simple notes on a board.  
4. Wider base holding.  
5. Lighter blocks stacking.  
6. Friends laughing with Blinky beeping.

## Episode 3 — Blinky’s Feelings Decoder
**Learning:** Asking is clearer than guessing. Empathy. Senses.

### Full dialogue draft
**Blinky:** I built a feelings calculator! Point and know!  
*(Points at Max)*  
**Blinky:** Result: happy.  
**Max:** I’m actually a bit frustrated.  
**Zara:** It said I’m sad but I’m excited!  
**Mia:** Faces give clues, but asking works better. “How do you feel?”  
**Ollie:** Our senses notice things, but words help us share.  
**Mia:** Let’s turn it into a listening light. It only lights when someone speaks.  
*(They share one real feeling each)*  
**Blinky:** Questions work better than guesses.  
**Closing:** Soft light stays open. No one has to be perfect.

### Storyboard beats
1. Blinky showing the calculator proudly.  
2. Wrong guesses, friends gently correcting.  
3. Mia demonstrating “How do you feel?”  
4. Machine becoming a listening light.  
5. Circle of friends sharing.  
6. Soft open light, smiles.
