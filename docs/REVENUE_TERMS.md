# PLEADING SANITY — CREATOR & STREETWEAR REVENUE TERMS

**Effective Date:** October 12, 2026
**Status:** Canonical Draft (Pre-Launch Lock)
**Motto:** LOVE OVER MONEY · EVOLUTION, NOT ERASURE · ONE SOURCE, ONE FAMILY

---

## 1. Core Commitment: Love Over Money

Pleading Sanity was founded by Shane Cooper as a grassroots survivor movement disguised as streetwear. We believe in radical financial transparency, community dignity, and zero exploitation.

We do not lock creators into predatory contracts, sell sensitive personal data, or extract profit from vulnerable users in mental distress.

---

## 2. Streetwear & Physical Merchandise: The 50 / 50 Split

For all official Pleading Sanity apparel — including the Crying Brain heavyweight hoodie, tracksuits, tees, and physical drops:

- **50% Production & Sustenance:** Covers ethical garment sourcing, heavyweight organic cotton milling, embroidery/screen-printing, packaging, shipping logistics, and baseline server maintenance required to keep the website online.
- **50% Grassroots Mental Health Causes:** Dispatched directly to frontline community mental health support, crisis lines, and suicide prevention initiatives.

**Quarterly Receipts:** Pleading Sanity commits to publishing quarterly summary receipts and allocation breakdowns publicly on **pleadingsanity.co.uk**. True service requires no smoke and mirrors.

---

## 3. Creator & Community Platform Revenue: The 90 / 10 Split

When independent artists, writers, musicians, and digital creators distribute or monetize original work through the Pleading Sanity platform:

- **90% Directly to the Creator:** You created the art, you lived the struggle, you own the value. 90% of net platform receipts from your work go directly into your pocket.
- **10% Platform Upkeep Fee:** Strictly funds server compute, bandwidth, transaction handling fees, and storage. Never extracted for executive bonuses or predatory margins.
- **100% Intellectual Property Ownership:** Creators retain complete copyright and ownership over their art, audio, and written work. Pleading Sanity receives only a non-exclusive license to display the work on the platform while you choose to host it here.
- **7-Day Takedown Right:** You may remove your content at any time. Upon receipt of a written takedown request, your work will be removed from public display within 7 business days.

---

## 4. Free Sanctuary Guarantee

Essential crisis resources — emergency phone numbers (Samaritans 116 123, NHS 111, Shout 85258), the Survivor Journal vault, and Arron's supportive baseline companion — will always remain **100% free of charge**.

We will never place human wellbeing or crisis intervention behind a paywall.

---

## 5. Legal Notice & Pre-Payout Eyeball

These terms represent our binding moral constitution. Prior to issuing monetary payouts to creators or executing public charity disbursements, these terms will be reviewed by independent legal counsel in England and Wales to ensure strict compliance with UK consumer protection, distance selling, and statutory tax obligations.
