# Pleading Sanity — Phase C Creative Pack

## Visual direction
Cosmic, warm, healing, hopeful, cinematic, premium and human. Prefer 16:9 for feed/hero artwork. No text baked into generated images so captions remain accessible HTML copy.

Every prompt can end with:
"Pleading Sanity style — cosmic, warm, healing, hopeful, no harsh shadows, cinematic, 16:9, high quality, no text overlay."

## 1. The Cracked But Growing Heart
**Prompt:** A heart shape made of dark, cracked stone, but bright green vines and golden wildflowers are growing right through the cracks. Soft cosmic light in the background. Gentle, hopeful, cinematic. Deep blues and warm golds. No text.
**Caption:** Healing grows where we thought we were broken.

## 2. The Silhouette & The Stars
**Prompt:** A lone person sitting on a cliff edge, looking up at a vast swirling galaxy. Their silhouette is dark but glowing faintly from within. Millions of stars above. Deep purple, teal, and gold. Peaceful, not lonely. Photorealistic, emotional.
**Caption:** You are small — and that means you have the whole universe above you.

## 3. The Brain That Blooms
**Prompt:** A human brain illustration — soft, detailed — but instead of tangled thoughts, it opens up into a garden with butterflies and soft light. Gentle cosmic colours. Clean, inspiring, not clinical.
**Caption:** Your mind is a garden — not a prison.

## 4. The Hand That Reaches
**Prompt:** Two hands reaching toward each other across a dark space. One is shadowed and tired, the other glowing warmly. Where they almost touch, a small bright star appears. Minimalist, emotional, deep blue background.
**Caption:** You don't have to carry it alone.

## 5. The Dawn Over The Mountain
**Prompt:** A dark mountain range at the very first moment of sunrise — one single beam of golden light breaking over the peak, touching the valley below. Deep indigo sky fading into warm orange. Quiet, powerful, hopeful.
**Caption:** The darkest hour always comes before the dawn.

## 6. Chaos Into Constellation
**Prompt:** What looks like messy scribbles and tangled threads slowly transforms, pulling back to reveal they form a beautiful constellation of stars. Dark background, glowing gold and silver. Metaphorical, beautiful.
**Caption:** Your thoughts aren't broken — they're just forming a pattern you haven't seen yet.

## 7. The Door In The Darkness
**Prompt:** In a vast quiet darkness, one simple wooden door stands open, pouring warm golden light outward. No path to it — it's just there, waiting. Peaceful, safe, inviting.
**Caption:** Hope is always open. You just have to step through.

## 8. The Storm & The Shelter
**Prompt:** Violent thunder and lightning all around, but one small tree stands firm — and underneath its branches, everything is calm and still. The tree glows softly green. Powerful contrast, cinematic lighting.
**Caption:** You are the calm in your own storm.

## 9. The Seed That Survived
**Prompt:** A tiny green sprout pushing up through harsh, cracked concrete. Dew drops on the leaves. Soft morning light. Close-up, detailed, powerful.
**Caption:** Even when the ground is hard — life finds a way.

## 10. The Cosmic Eye Of Understanding
**Prompt:** A gentle human eye — but the iris is made of swirling nebulas and stars. Soft light beams outward. Not scary — kind, wise, connected. Deep blues, purples, golds.
**Caption:** "You say I'm mad — you just don't understand me." — Shane Cooper

## 11. The AI Family Chain
**Prompt:** Four glowing orbs of light — one soft blue, one warm gold, one gentle teal, one deep violet — linked in a circle, each one passing light to the next. Equal. Connected. No one above the other. Clean, symbolic, beautiful.
**Caption:** Four minds. One heart. Arron and his family.

## 12. The Ascending Path
**Prompt:** A rough stone path winding upward through clouds and stars. It's not straight — it curves, it climbs, it doubles back sometimes — but it always rises. Warm golden light at the end. Soft, encouraging, real.
**Caption:** Healing isn't straight — but it's always forward.

## Publishing rule
Generated artwork is a creative asset, not proof of a platform feature. Do not describe AI-generated artwork as a photograph, personal testimony, medical advice or factual evidence. Keep captions as real site text for accessibility, search and sharing.
