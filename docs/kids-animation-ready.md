# Keep Kids Sane — Animation-Ready Pilot Episodes
**Status:** ANIMATION-READY DRAFT · Ages 3–9 · Timed screenplays + shot lists · Not filmed · Not uploaded  
**Characters:** Ollie the Owl · Zara the Squirrel · Max · Mia · Blinky the Robot  
**Target runtime:** ~3–4 minutes per episode  
**Safety:** Separate from adult community. Gentle only. Parents can pause. No private child data.

## Shared production bible
- Background: deep night sky `#000103` with soft stars and faint cyan/purple nebula. Calm, never busy.
- Lighting: soft frontal + gentle cyan rim. No harsh shadows.
- Character scale: large heads, rounded bodies, big expressive eyes.
- Movement: slow and clear. No fast cuts for the youngest viewers.
- Music: soft acoustic guitar + light pad. Blinky has a short friendly beep motif.
- Subtitles: always on, large, high-contrast frost text.
- Closing song style: simple, 4 lines, singable, repeated once.

## Episode 1 — The Big Breath Balloon
**Runtime target:** 3:40  
**Learning:** Air takes up space. Slowing down helps. Teamwork.

### Timed screenplay
**0:00–0:20** Opening. Soft sky. Ollie stands under a simple festival banner.  
**Ollie (warm, slow):** Welcome to the Great Sky Festival. Today we see whose balloon can float the highest.  
**0:20–0:45** Zara holds a colourful balloon, eyes bright. Max leans forward, cape flopping.  
**Zara (excited):** Mine’s ready! Look at the colours!  
**Max (eager):** Launch it now! I want to see it go!  
**0:45–1:05** Blinky’s light blinks. He starts counting.  
**Blinky (beepy):** Counting… three… two…  
**Mia (calm, lantern soft):** Wait. Let’s check it first.  
**1:05–1:30** Balloon sags and sits flat. Soft disappointed faces. No shame.  
**Zara (quiet):** Oh… it fell.  
**Max (disappointed, not angry):** It didn’t even lift.  
**1:30–2:10** Mia raises the lantern. Group takes three soft breaths together.  
**Mia:** Let’s stop and notice our breath. In… and out. Three soft breaths.  
*(Visual: gentle chest rise. Optional for viewers.)*  
**2:10–2:40** Ollie points at the balloon.  
**Ollie:** Air fills the balloon. If there’s a little hole, the air sneaks out.  
**Zara (finding it):** Here! A tiny hole!  
**2:40–3:10** They patch it together. Balloon rises gently.  
**Max:** We can fix that.  
**All (soft cheer):** It works!  
**3:10–3:40** Friends look up, smiling. Not racing.  
**Ollie:** The highest part was helping each other.  
**Closing song (sung softly):** One breath, one try, one friend at a time. The sky is big enough for everyone.

### Voice direction
- Ollie: warm, unhurried, curious.  
- Zara: bright but not shouty.  
- Max: eager, then softens.  
- Mia: clear and calm.  
- Blinky: short beeps between words, never loud.  
- Breathing: optional, never forced.

### Sound & music
- Soft whoosh when balloon sags.  
- Gentle patch sound (cloth + soft stick).  
- Light rising whoosh on second try.  
- Closing song: acoustic + soft pad, 20 seconds.

### Subtitle / caption script
Full dialogue above, plus on-screen: “Air fills the balloon.” “Three soft breaths.” “Helping each other.”

### Parent notes
Air takes up space. A small hole lets air out. Slowing down and checking together helps. Breathing is optional and gentle. The win is teamwork, not height.

## Episode 2 — Zara’s Brilliant Mistake
**Runtime target:** 3:30  
**Learning:** One change at a time. Mistakes can teach.

### Timed screenplay
**0:00–0:25** Zara presents a block stacker, proud.  
**Zara:** Watch my new stacker! It should make the tallest tower ever!  
**0:25–0:40** Max leans in. Tower tumbles.  
**Max:** Do it fast!  
**Zara (embarrassed, not ashamed):** It broke.  
**0:40–1:10** Mia steps in calmly.  
**Mia:** Let’s try one change at a time.  
**Ollie:** What if the base is wider?  
**1:10–1:50** They test. Wider base holds better. Blinky notes it.  
**Blinky:** Base wider… data good.  
**1:50–2:30** Lighter blocks. Stack improves.  
**Zara:** Now lighter blocks?  
**2:30–3:00** Mia summarises.  
**Mia:** The mistake showed us what to try next.  
**Blinky:** Mistake = data!  
**All:** (gentle laugh)  
**3:00–3:30** High-five the learning. Closing line: “One change at a time.”

### Shot list
1. Zara presenting (proud)  
2. Tower fall (soft)  
3. Group around a simple note board  
4. Wider base test  
5. Lighter blocks test  
6. Laugh + Blinky beep  
7. Soft high-five

### Parent notes
Trying one change at a time is how science works. Mistakes give information. No pressure for a perfect tower.

## Episode 3 — Blinky’s Feelings Decoder
**Runtime target:** 3:30  
**Learning:** Asking is clearer than guessing. Empathy.

### Timed screenplay
**0:00–0:30** Blinky shows a small device.  
**Blinky:** I built a feelings calculator! Point and know!  
**0:30–1:00** He points. Wrong guesses. Friends gently correct.  
**Max:** I’m actually a bit frustrated.  
**Zara:** It said I’m sad but I’m excited!  
**1:00–1:40** Mia demonstrates.  
**Mia:** Faces give clues, but asking works better. How do you feel?  
**1:40–2:20** They turn the device into a listening light. It only glows when someone speaks.  
**Mia:** It only lights when someone speaks.  
**2:20–3:00** Each friend shares one real feeling. Soft circle.  
**3:00–3:30** Blinky learns. Soft open light.  
**Blinky:** Questions work better than guesses.  
**Closing:** “No one has to be perfect.”

### Shot list
1. Blinky proud with device  
2. Wrong guess close-ups  
3. Mia asking gently  
4. Device becomes listening light  
5. Circle share  
6. Soft open light

### Parent notes
Guessing feelings can be wrong. Asking “How do you feel?” is clearer. Listening comes first. No one has to perform an emotion.
