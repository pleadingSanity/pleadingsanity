# Insane Radio — Artist guidelines

## What we do
- Link to official streams, buy pages, and artist channels.
- Never host copyrighted audio on our server.
- Display artist name, title, year, and support link on every track.
- Show the Artist Promise and contact email on the radio page.

## Adding a track
Edit /data/radio-library.json. Add:
{ "era", "region", "genre", "title", "artist", "year", "youtubeId" or "", "supportLink" }

## Removal
If an artist or label objects, remove the track immediately. No arguments.
