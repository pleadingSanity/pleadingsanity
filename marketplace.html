Legal & buyer protection

Prohibited items: the following may never be listed — illegal goods, weapons (including replicas and components), drugs and drug paraphernalia, hate content, non-consensual or intimate content, counterfeit goods, stolen items, and anything that violates UK law. Listings are reviewed and may be removed.

Age-gating: items for adults only (18+) must be clearly marked. Buyers confirm they are 18 or over at checkout for age-gated listings.

UK consumer rights: physical goods are covered by the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations — you have a 14-day cancellation / return window from delivery. Digital downloads are exempt from the 14-day cooling-off period once download begins, and this is stated at purchase.

Data & GDPR: we only share your name, email and delivery address with the seller needed to fulfil your order. Payment is processed by PayPal (and soon Stripe) — we never store your card details. Read our privacy policy.

Seller terms: by listing, you agree to the community guidelines and seller terms. You are responsible for accurate descriptions, fulfilment and UK tax on your sales.
