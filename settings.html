🗑️ Delete my account

This deletes your account and what it holds from our live database: Sanity Profile, Passport, story, journal, status, posts, comments, hearts and votes, friendships, creations, writing for the site, uploaded images, your activity record and your conversations with Arron. It can't be undone. (Your right to erasure under UK GDPR.)

Our hosting provider may hold routine backups for a limited time, which we can't erase on demand. Words you already sent to an AI (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI or the OpenRouter backups) were handled under their policies and can't be recalled by us.
