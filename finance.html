What this is — and what it isn't

It is

A free education, calculator, comparison and preparation tool. You stay in charge and deal with the provider directly.

It isn't

A lender, a credit broker or a regulated financial adviser. Pleading Sanity earns no commission here and sends nobody your details.

Three different things

Planning estimate our maths on your figures · Provider eligibility the provider's own check · Lender decision only the lender makes it.

How to read reviews (and why they're kept separate)
Customer feedback

What people say about service. Check the source, how many reviews, how recent, and whether the provider controls the review page.

Regulatory status

Whether the firm is authorised, and as a lender or broker. Only the FCA Register confirms this.

Finance suitability

Whether the product fits your budget and circumstances. Great reviews don't make a product right for you.

Price

APR, fees and total repayable. A poorly reviewed firm can still have a fair product, and a loved one an expensive product.

12 · THE RULE


You stay in control




Compare total cost
APR, total repayable, fees, deposit, final payment and early-settlement terms.


Soft search first
Eligibility checks show your likelihood without a mark other lenders can see. A full application usually leaves a hard search.


No fake approvals
Only the lender decides. Nobody can guarantee approval — walk away from anyone who does.


Never invent anything
False income, edited documents or hidden debts are fraud, and can leave you far worse off.




Your data


Your profile, quotes, tracker and documents live in this browser's storage only. Pleading Sanity has no copy. Clearing it here removes it from this device.
