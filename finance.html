08 · PEOPLE & PROVIDERS

Find help without handing over control

We will only present a lender, broker, debt adviser or review source as verified when its identity, regulatory status and current information can be checked. We will never invent a “bad-credit specialist” or rank a company because it pays more.

Bad-credit route

Look for firms that explicitly state their acceptance criteria, check their FCA status and compare fees before sharing documents.

Debt difficulty

If borrowing is becoming the solution to existing debt, pause and consider free debt guidance first.

Reviews

Reviews are evidence to inspect, not proof of suitability. Check independent sources and recent patterns, not one headline score.

Escalation

If a lender declines you, ask what criterion failed and whether the decision can be explained or appealed. The GBT cannot see confidential underwriting rules.

You stay in control
