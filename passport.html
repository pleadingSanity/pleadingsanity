🛂 Sanity Passport

Who you are, in your own words, and only what you want to share. It's voluntary. It's not a medical record, a diagnosis or a score.

How it works

  • Every field starts private. Only you see it until you choose Members (signed-in people) or Public (anyone, on your profile page).
  • Arron reads nothing unless you say yes twice: tick “Arron may use this” on a field and turn on the switch below. Arron uses it to understand you, and doesn't quote it back unless you ask.
  • Whatever reaches Arron is sent to the AI provider that answers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, or an OpenRouter backup). Privacy details →
  • You can download all your data, or delete this Passport at any time.

Please don't write addresses, phone numbers, your school or workplace, or private things about other people. Visible fields pass the same kindness check as a post.
