EVOLUTION, NOT ERASURE

Human + AI, without losing the human.

Pleading Sanity is being built as a human-first ecosystem: healing, creativity, games, community, philosophy and AI in one house. AI assists. Humans decide.

Three modes

HUMAN MODE

Your words, choices and judgement lead. No AI is required.

AI MODE

Machine-generated help is clearly labelled. AI is not an authority and never pretends to be a person.

TOGETHER MODE

Human and AI create, compare, question and improve something together. The human remains the final decision-maker.

Truth Lab

No infallible truth engine. Every contribution can be framed honestly:

KNOWN

Supported by evidence or independently verifiable information.

EXPERIENCE

Something personally experienced by the person sharing it.

THOUGHT

An idea, question or hypothesis — not established fact.

BELIEF

A personal, philosophical or spiritual belief.

UNKNOWN

Something we genuinely do not know yet.

Sanity Passport

A voluntary profile should describe a person, not diagnose them. The intended model is:

Who I am · What matters · What inspires me · What I'm learning · What I'm creating · What helps me · My goals · My story.

Privacy controls must be first-class: public/private visibility, explicit AI-memory consent, export and deletion. Until those controls are enforced end-to-end, the platform must not claim that profile data is private, encrypted or invisible to AI.

Pleading Sanity's user-facing companion. Arron is an AI, not Shane and not a therapist.

Connection rule: a provider is called connected only when its API/key/permission is actually available on the deployed system. A documented role is not proof of a live integration.

Community without clout

Contribution matters more than popularity. Possible roles include Storyteller, Guardian, Supporter, Survivor, Thinker, Creator, Voice, Grower, Connector and Pioneer.

Guardians welcome, protect, report, signpost and connect. They are not therapists, police or secret moderators.

The Sanity Arena can host rap, poetry, art, music, storytelling, philosophy, comedy and Human × AI challenges with published judging criteria rather than hidden popularity ranking.

Keep Kids Sane

Children need a separate safety boundary, not a smaller adult social network. The target architecture focuses on emotional intelligence, confidence, kindness, resilience, creativity, critical thinking, digital safety and age-appropriate AI literacy.

Roadmap

NOW

Protect working games, finance and sanctuary features. Make AI/provider claims accurate. Establish Truth Lab and Human + AI principles.

NEXT

Harden the Sanity Passport, AI-memory consent, Human/AI/Together labelling and contribution roles.

THEN

Expand Sanity Feed, Survivors Wall, Guardians, Arena and richer creation tools with real moderation and account controls.

VISION

A durable human-first network where technology increases connection, creativity and agency rather than replacing them.

Developer & partner door

Build with the existing vanilla HTML/CSS/JS platform, Netlify functions and database. Preserve working features. Add infrastructure only when the feature genuinely needs it.

Partners can contribute technology, moderation, creative tools, research, accessibility, youth safety or community support without owning the truth of the movement.
